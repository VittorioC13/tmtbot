The Coca-Cola Company (The Coca-Cola Company (NYSE: KO)) is systematically rolling out a unified global packaging design across its core brands, aiming to streamline visual identity and reduce supply chain friction worldwide. Consistency across international product lines is hardly a novel concept in fast-moving consumer goods, but implementing a uniform packaging blueprint on a multi-billion-unit scale presents immense operational hurdles. By removing fragmented regional designs, The Coca-Cola Company (NYSE: KO) eliminates redundant creative expenditures across disparate operating groups.
