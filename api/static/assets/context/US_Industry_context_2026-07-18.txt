The technology enables financial institutions to confidently assess and extend credit to borrowers who lack a formal credit history by leveraging an expanded pool of alternative behavioural and transactional data.

However, under traditional credit infrastructure, these achievements remain invisible to new lenders.

Mathesis addresses this challenge through the concept of ‘Personal Equity’, the quantified expression of an individual’s financial behaviour aggregated across every institution with which they have transacted. Industry Highlights

The report identifies several major trends transforming aerospace and defense manufacturing nationwide:

Announced U.S. industry investment has more than doubled between 2022-2023 and 2024-2025, while project announcements have nearly tripled, signaling the start of a new defense-driven growth cycle.

The industry is shifting from an innovation-first model to one centered on production readiness, manufacturing scale, and supply chain resilience, fundamentally changing how companies evaluate future investment locations.

Workforce and supplier constraints have replaced demand uncertainty as the industry's primary growth barrier, making industrial capacity a key competitive advantage.

Among the report's most notable findings is that aerospace and defense manufacturers no longer face a shortage of demand. Instead, the industry's greatest obstacle has become the ability to meet it.
