[TITLE]Stock Market Highlights, June 12: Sensex, Nifty surge nearly 2% as falling oil prices lift market sentiment:

Honasa Consumer (Add, FV: ₹455) At its maiden Investor Day, Honasa’s management outlined its FY2031 vision of revenues of ₹5,500 crore+, implying an 18 per cent CAGR over FY2026-31E, and a 500 basis points-plus expansion in EBITDA margin to 15 per cent+, translating into a 28 per cent EBITDA CAGR. The company’s strategy of concentrating A&P investments on focus categories while strengthening its direct distribution network has put Mamaearth back on a growth trajectory, with broad-based momentum across channels. Management expressed confidence in its increasingly refined brand-building and scaling playbooks and appears well-positioned to drive growth across existing brands while expanding into new categories such as nutraceuticals and fragrances. We have increased EPS estimates by 6-8 per cent and revised the fair value to ₹455 (from ₹405), based on 46x June 2028E PE (45x earlier).

Equitas Small Finance Bank (Buy, FV: ₹95) Equitas SFB hosted an analyst day in Mumbai yesterday to showcase its business strategy and leadership team. Management shared a five-year outlook suggesting a 21 per cent advances CAGR and a steady-state RoA of 1.5 per cent. Asset quality has shown negligible signs of stress so far from the West Asia conflict, while business growth remains strong. We continue to like Equitas as a play on the benign credit environment and retain our Buy rating with a fair value of ₹95 (₹90 earlier).

Electric Utilities and Real Estate (Sector View: Cautious) India’s data centre infrastructure stands at 1.6 GW and remains at a nascent stage, with medium-term growth targets of 15 GW by FY2035 — still a fraction of the current capacity in the US (40 GW). Data centres represent a growing opportunity with power demand of 150 billion units by FY2035E, or 6 per cent of overall demand, and will require real estate investment of 160 million sq ft (12 per cent of office space in FY2035E). Utilities such as Clean Max and Adani Green, which cater directly to such industrial demand, are likely to be beneficiaries, while real estate companies such as Lodha, Mindspace REIT, DLF and Anant Raj are looking to capitalise through land sales and leasing of constructed power shells.

Pharmaceuticals (Sector View: Attractive) The Indian Pharma Market reported robust 12.1 per cent year-on-year growth in May 2026, marking double-digit growth for the sixth consecutive month. Excluding GLP-1, we estimate May 2026 sales still grew around 11 per cent year-on-year. IPM growth in the MAT May 2026 period stood at 10.7 per cent year-on-year. While pricing contributed the bulk of IPM growth in MAT May 2026, the contribution from volumes increased to a healthy 320 basis points versus 80 basis points in MAT May 2025. Led by continued benefits from pricing and new launches, along with acquisitions and in-licensing deals, we bake in 10-18 per cent year-on-year (ex-TRP) domestic sales growth for our coverage in FY2027E. TRP, Cipla, Sun Pharma, Mankind, Emcure and Corona are our preferred picks in the space.
