China's stock markets fell on Thursday as a deepening selloff in technology shares overshadowed the US Federal Reserve's decision to keep interest rates unchanged, Reuters reported.At the midday break, the benchmark Shanghai Composite Index was down 0.9%, while the blue-chip CSI300 Index declined 1.2%. Technology stocks led the losses, with the ChiNext Composite Index falling 3.6% and Shanghai's tech-focused STAR50 Index tumbling 4.2%.The CSI 5G Communications Index plunged 6.1% during morning trading, highlighting the pressure on technology-related stocks.Robot maker Yushu Technology, also known as Unitree, slumped 4.3% after its third-quarter net profit declined despite revenue rising 70% from a year earlier. Investors were assessing whether strong AI-related spending could continue to support technology companies' earnings growth.The Federal Reserve kept interest rates unchanged on Wednesday, as expected, delaying any changes to borrowing costs while awaiting further inflation data. According to Reuters, the decision came ahead of next week's US midterm elections, with the central bank indicating that inflation remained elevated.In Hong Kong, the benchmark Hang Seng Index edged up 0.1%, while the city's technology shares fell 2%, Reuters reported.The contrasting performance highlighted continued pressure on technology stocks as investors reassessed AI-related valuations and growth expectations, even as the broader regional market outlook remained sensitive to corporate earnings and US monetary policy.(Disclaimer: This article is based on inputs from agencies.
