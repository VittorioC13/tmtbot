- **AI purchasing autonomy:** 3% are comfortable allowing AI to make purchases automatically.
- **Holiday e-commerce:** Expected to grow 8–10% year over year.
- **Retail spending:** Expected to grow approximately 4–5% across general merchandise, household goods, and food.
- **Airfares:** Fares are up more than 20% year over year.

@@@ Main Insights:
- **Higher prices are driving much of the expected spending increase:** Consumers may spend more in dollars without buying more items.
- **Inflation is squeezing both sides of the spending outlook:** It is cited by consumers planning to spend more and by those cutting holiday budgets.
- **AI is increasingly used for deal discovery and price comparison:** These tools may support additional purchases by helping consumers find savings and products, but shoppers largely want to retain control of checkout.
- **Shipping data show limited evidence of a volume rebound:** Freight data have not confirmed a meaningful pickup in shipping volumes.
- **Travel demand remains comparatively resilient:** Consumers continue to prioritize travel despite sharply higher fares.

- **Michelle Weaver, Morgan Stanley:** “This year holiday sales may rise even if shoppers leave with fewer bags.”
- **Michelle Weaver, Morgan Stanley:** The key test of consumer strength is “how much more people buy, not how much more they pay.”
