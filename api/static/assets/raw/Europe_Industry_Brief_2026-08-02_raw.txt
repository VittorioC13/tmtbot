The global in-orbit servicing market is projected to grow significantly as private and governmental entities seek to extend the life of their space assets.

@@@ Key Companies:

- **Trend Explanation:** The rise of artificial intelligence in defense applications is reshaping military operations, particularly in autonomous systems and data management. Companies are investing in infrastructure to train AI systems for defense applications, which is critical given the increasing complexity of modern warfare. The market for defense AI is expected to expand rapidly as nations prioritize technological advancements in military capabilities.

@@@ Key Companies:

  - **Anduril:** Known for its advanced defense technologies, Anduril is likely to leverage Agon’s infrastructure for its autonomous systems, enhancing its operational capabilities in the defense sector.

- **Defence AI Infrastructure:** The defense sector is witnessing increased funding and interest, particularly in AI-driven solutions. The geopolitical climate is pushing governments to invest heavily in defense technologies, creating a fertile ground for startups like Agon.
- **Direct Lending:** The direct lending market is characterized by competition among private equity firms and alternative lenders. As traditional banks tighten lending standards, direct lenders are filling the gap, leading to increased deal flow.
