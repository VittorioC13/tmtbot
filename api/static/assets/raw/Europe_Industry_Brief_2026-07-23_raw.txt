#### Critical Minerals

- **Trend Explanation:** The U.S. government's push to reduce reliance on China for critical minerals is reshaping the mining industry. Critical minerals are essential for defense technologies, and the market is poised for growth due to increased federal investment and strategic initiatives aimed at securing supply chains.

@@@ Key Companies:
  - **USA Rare Earth:** This company is actively involved in the production of rare earth elements crucial for military applications. With significant federal investment, USA Rare Earth is well-positioned to capitalize on the growing demand for domestic mineral production.
  - **MP Materials:** Operating the Mountain Pass mine in California, MP Materials is a key player in the rare earth supply chain. The Pentagon's investment in the company underscores its strategic importance in bolstering U.S. defense capabilities.

@@@ Key Companies:

  - **General Electric (GE):** GE is leveraging 3D printing in its manufacturing processes, particularly in aerospace components. The company's investments in additive manufacturing technology are enhancing production efficiency and reducing costs.
