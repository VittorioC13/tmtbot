@@@ Subsector Breakdown:
- **Pharmaceuticals:** The pharmaceutical subsector is experiencing growth driven by innovations in drug development and the rise of precision medicine. Companies are increasingly utilizing AI for drug discovery, which enhances research and development efficiency. For example, the global AI in Drug Repurposing Market is projected to grow from approximately USD 1.28 billion in 2025 to around USD 10.56 billion by 2035, reflecting a CAGR of 23.4%.

- **Healthcare Services:** The healthcare services space is evolving, with major players like UnitedHealth acquiring digital health startups to integrate telemedicine solutions. This consolidation trend is indicative of the sector's shift towards more integrated care models.
