@@@ Main Insights:
- **Higher prices are driving nominal spending growth:** Many consumers expect to spend more because goods cost more, not because they plan to buy more items.
- **Inflation is pressuring both sides of the spending outlook:** It is cited by consumers planning to spend more and by those cutting their holiday budgets.
- **AI is becoming a shopping and discovery tool:** Consumers primarily use it to find deals and compare prices, but remain reluctant to delegate purchasing decisions.
- **Retail growth is expected to be uneven:** eCommerce is forecast to grow faster than spending across general merchandise, household goods, and food.
- **Freight data do not yet show a meaningful pickup in shipping volumes:** This supports the distinction between higher spending and an increase in goods purchased.
- **Travel remains a relative bright spot:** Consumers continue prioritizing air travel despite substantially higher fares.
